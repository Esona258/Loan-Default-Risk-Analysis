# Credit Risk Analysis – Loan Default Insights

## 📊 Overview
Analysis of 600+ personal loans (2024–2025) to identify key drivers of loan default and support risk reduction for a financial institution experiencing a ~25% default rate (target: 12%).

## 🎯 Business Objective
Understand borrower risk patterns and identify features that increase likelihood of default to improve credit approval decisions and reduce portfolio risk.

## 🛠 Tools
Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Jupyter Notebook

## 🔍 Approach
- Data cleaning and preprocessing  
- Exploratory data analysis (EDA)  
- Risk segmentation by credit score, income, DTI, employment, and loan purpose  
- Visualization of default patterns across borrower groups  

## 📌 Key Insights
- Lower credit scores are strongly associated with higher default risk  
- High debt-to-income (DTI) significantly increases probability of default  
- Short employment history is a strong risk indicator  
- Certain loan purposes consistently show elevated default rates  

## 💡 Business Impact
Findings support tighter credit policies, improved risk segmentation, and targeted lending strategies aimed at reducing default rates toward the 12% business target.
