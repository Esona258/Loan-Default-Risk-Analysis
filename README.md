# Credit Risk Analysis – Loan Default Insights

## Overview

Analyzed 600+ personal loans (2024–2025) to understand drivers of loan default and support risk reduction efforts for a financial institution with a ~25% default rate (target: 12%).

## Tools

Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Jupyter Notebook

## Key Work

* Calculated overall default rate and segment-level risk
* Analyzed risk patterns across credit score, DTI, employment length, and loan purpose
* Built visual insights to highlight high-risk borrower groups

## Key Insights

* Lower credit scores strongly correlate with higher default risk
* Higher debt-to-income (DTI) increases probability of default
* Short employment history is associated with elevated risk
* Certain loan purposes show consistently higher default rates

## Impact

Findings support improved credit decisioning, better risk segmentation, and potential reduction of portfolio default rates toward business targets.
